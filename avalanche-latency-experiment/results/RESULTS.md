# Results
> **Provenance: SIMULATED.** These numbers come from the reference model in `alp.simulate`, executed with the pre-registered protocol. They demonstrate that the analysis pipeline is complete and reproducible. They are **not** measurements of the cyber range and must not be reported as experimental results. Section *Outstanding data* lists what a real campaign has to supply.

Campaign profile `demo`: 225 runs (5 configurations x 3 topologies x 5 loads x 3 repeats), 5 s warm-up, 20 s measurement, 5 s drain, 697,500 scheduled transactions in the measurement windows, 1.88 h of minimum machine time. Load is generated by 25 workstations replaying immutable traces; the inferential unit is the run.

## Key findings
- On T0 local the best static configuration is **C4 (250 ms)**, stable up to 400 tx/s with a mean p99 of 278 ms (lowest mean p99).
- On T1 physical VPN the best static configuration is **C4 (250 ms)**, stable up to 400 tx/s with a mean p99 of 345 ms (lowest mean p99).
- On T2 three-region (emulated) the best static configuration is **C3 (500 ms)**, stable up to 400 tx/s with a mean p99 of 793 ms (lowest mean p99).
- The largest paired improvement of the primary endpoint is 29575 ms (95 % CI 29550 to 29600 ms) for C4 (250 ms) on T0 local at 400 tx/s.
- Largest load that stays stable across every topology, strict rule (all repeats) / majority rule: C0 (stock) 100 / 100 tx/s; C1 (1000 ms) 200 / 200 tx/s; C2 (750 ms) 400 / 400 tx/s; C3 (500 ms) 400 / 400 tx/s; C4 (250 ms) 25 / 400 tx/s (the campaign offered up to 400 tx/s).
- The two pre-registered rules disagree in 1 config-topology combinations (C4 (250 ms) on T2 three-region (emulated)). Those cells sit on the stability boundary and are exactly where the precision rule calls for additional repeats before a claim is made.
- 10 of 75 cells failed at least one pre-registered criterion of Tab. 13; the run-level reasons are in `run_stability.csv` under `failed_criteria`.
- Shorter pacing costs CPU even when the chain is nearly idle: at 25 tx/s the validator CPU p95 rises from 14 % (C1 (1000 ms)) to 28 % (C4 (250 ms)), which is the mechanism behind RQ2.
- Convergence between the two independent read nodes (eq. 7) has a p99 of 25 ms on T0 local, 28 ms on T1 physical VPN, 150 ms on T2 three-region (emulated); the floor of 25 ms is the client polling grid, not a property of the network.
