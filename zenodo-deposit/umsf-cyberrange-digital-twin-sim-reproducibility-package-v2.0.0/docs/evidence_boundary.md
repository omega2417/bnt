# Evidence boundary / Межа доказовості

*This is the normative statement of what this deposit does and does not prove.
It takes precedence over any looser wording found elsewhere in the package or in
the related manuscript.*

## 1. English

All numerical results in this deposit are outputs of a software model under
explicitly stated assumptions. They are **not** measurements of the physical UMSF
cyber range and do not confirm actual WAN, VPN or automatic-transfer-switch
switchover times, Wi-Fi coverage or capacity, power-source autonomy, battery
thermal behaviour, or the field accuracy of detectors.

The deposit demonstrates the **executability, internal verification and
reproducibility** of a software artifact. It does **not** demonstrate calibration
of the model against a physical object, sim-to-real transfer, or operational
fitness.

## 2. Українською

Усі числові результати цього депозиту є виходами програмної моделі за явно
заданих припущень. Вони **не є вимірюваннями** фізичного кіберполігону УМСФ і не
підтверджують фактичний час перемикання WAN, VPN чи АВР, покриття або ємність
Wi-Fi, автономність джерел живлення, температурні режими акумуляторної системи
чи польову точність детекторів.

Депозит підтверджує **виконуваність, внутрішню верифікацію та відтворюваність**
програмного артефакту. Він **не підтверджує** калібрування моделі відносно
фізичного об'єкта, sim-to-real перенесення або експлуатаційну придатність.

## 3. Maturity level of this release

| Execution mode | Status in release 2.0.0 |
|---|---|
| `SIM` — pure software simulation | **implemented; the only mode exercised here** |
| `EMU` — emulation of devices and protocols | not implemented |
| `REPLAY` — replay of recorded real telemetry | no real telemetry exists to replay |
| `HIL` — hardware in the loop | **blocked in software** by four UNKNOWN parameters |
| Real-Test — physical trial | not performed |

Maturity: M1 / S0-smoke. The correct name of the artifact at this level is
*software prototype of a digital twin* or *simulation-based pre-experimental
model* — not "a validated digital twin of the physical range".

## 4. Vocabulary used in this package

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| Physical experiment | Trial on real equipment with primary logs, timestamps, named operator roles and verifiable measurements. **Not performed.** |
| Remotely operated physical experiment | Physical equipment and local logging exist; the researcher triggers scenarios or retrieves logs over a secure channel. **Not performed.** |
| Emulation | Reproduction of the behaviour of a network, device or protocol in a controlled environment. **Not performed.** |
| Simulation | Computational reproduction of processes from stated rules and parameters. Its outputs are not observations. **This is what was done.** |
| Synthetic data | Records generated by software. They may not be called data of a physical range, of an enterprise, of users or of real sensors. **This is what this deposit contains.** |
| Digital twin | Used here only as *software prototype of a digital twin*, because no calibration or synchronisation with a physical counterpart exists. |
| Verification | The implementation matches its specification. **Demonstrated.** |
| Calibration | Model parameters fitted to real telemetry. **Not performed.** |
| Physical validation | Model behaviour tested against the real system. **Not performed.** |

## 5. Parameter provenance

| Evidence status | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `MEASURED` | 0 | Confirmed by a measurement on the real object |
| `VENDOR_SPEC` | 0 | Confirmed by a device datasheet |
| `DERIVED` | 0 | Computed from stronger evidence |
| `ASSUMED` | 0 | Explicitly assumed, with a rationale |
| `SYNTHETIC_DEMO` | 194 | Chosen for a demonstration model, no external evidence |
| `UNKNOWN` | 4 | Not known; may not be invented |

The four UNKNOWN parameters are `power.site_a.chemistry`,
`power.site_a.parallel_count`, `vpn.mtu` and `vpn.protocol`. They are not a
defect of the implementation but the correct state of knowledge, and they block
`HIL` in software until they are measured.

## 6. Statements this deposit does NOT support

The following must not be written in any paper, report or record that cites this
archive, unless a separate evidence package of primary logs is produced first:

* any count of physically instrumented runs;
* real measurements of network, Wi-Fi, VPN or power infrastructure;
* a deployed distributed-ledger or blockchain testbed and its measured
  throughput, latency or resource use;
* field precision, recall or detection latency of any detector;
* actual autonomy in minutes of any battery or power station;
* actual WAN, VPN or ATS switchover times;
* "the digital twin of the physical range was validated";
* "independently reproduced", where the same team repeated its own run.

## 7. What would be needed to lift each restriction

1. Physical inventory of every parameter that is currently `SYNTHETIC_DEMO` or
   `UNKNOWN`, with datasheets or measurements.
2. A passive real baseline collected before any intervention.
3. Real calibration data and an untouched holdout sample for validation.
4. A frozen model version and a pre-registered protocol with primary endpoints
   and exclusion rules defined in advance.
5. EMU and REPLAY implementations, then HIL under the safety gates.
6. A pre-defined physical bridge experiment, then a blind real test, then shadow
   mode.

Only after step 6 may claims about actual switchover times, coverage, autonomy or
field detector accuracy be made.
