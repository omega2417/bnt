# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: treat other participants with respect, assume good faith, criticize
work rather than people, and keep scientific disagreement scientific — with
evidence, reproducible examples and references.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

Reports go to the maintainer listed in `CITATION.cff`. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer will respond as promptly as circumstances
allow.
